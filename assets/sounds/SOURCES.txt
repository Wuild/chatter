Whispr loudness adjustment (2026-10-08):
All ten files have been re-encoded as Vorbis quality 6 after gain adjustment and
lookahead peak limiting. Original sample rates, channels and durations are retained.
The loudest 100 ms RMS window targets -12 dBFS (within 1 dB), avoiding quiet tails
skewing the level of short pings. Decoded 192 kHz oversampled peaks were checked
below -0.5 dBFS. The limiter uses a -3 dB ceiling before Vorbis encoding to allow
for reconstruction overshoot. This improves consistency but different timbres and
durations can still sound different in-game.
Rebuild from original, unprocessed sources using:
python3 tools/normalize_sounds.py ORIGINAL_DIRECTORY OUTPUT_DIRECTORY
Do not use these processed files as the input for repeated re-encoding.
