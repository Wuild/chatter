# Chatter

Keep your **World of Warcraft whispers** together in one inbox or give each
conversation its own window. Chatter includes saved history, unread badges,
class colors, smileys, and character and Battle.net conversations.

## Get started

[Download the source ZIP](https://github.com/Wuild/chatter/archive/refs/heads/main.zip),
extract it, and rename the addon folder to **Chatter**. Place it in
**Interface/AddOns** and restart the game.

1. Open Chatter with **/chatter** or its minimap button.
2. Whisper someone using **/w** or the game's usual Whisper menu.
3. Open **Settings** with the cog or **/chatter settings** to make it your own.

Includes support for Retail, Classic Era, Anniversary/TBC, Wrath, Cataclysm,
Mists, and Forever. Use one whisper-window addon at a time to avoid conflicts.

## Chat controls

- **Enter** sends your message. **Shift-click an item** to insert its link.
- **Click a conversation** to switch chats. Your unfinished draft stays there.
- **Scroll up** to read older messages; **Jump to latest** returns to new ones.
- **Drag across message text**, then press **Ctrl+C** to copy it.
- **Open the conversation menu** to move a chat into its own window or dock it again.
- **Drag the title bar** to move a window and its bottom-right corner to resize it.
  Window positions, sizes, and undocked conversations are remembered.
- **Escape** dismisses an open panel, then closes the chat window.

## Make it yours

Choose a theme, adjust fonts and opacity, pick a notification sound—including
classic WIM sounds—and set your preferred window sizes. Combat options control
when windows hide or stay closed. Settings are shared across your characters.

Under **Extensions**, you can enable and configure:

- **Notifications** — small alerts for whispers while the chat window is hidden,
  with a movable position and adjustable appearance.
- **Emoji** — a smiley picker and inline text smileys.
- **Keyword actions** — clickable words such as `[invite]`, with optional automatic
  invites restricted to friends or guild members.
- **Character names** — class colors for known names mentioned in messages.
